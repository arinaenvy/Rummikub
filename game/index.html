Game log
    How to play · rules and controls

    Goal: empty your rack before the computer.

    Groups: 3 or 4 equal numbers, all different colors. Example: red 8, blue 8, black 8.

    Runs: 3 or more consecutive numbers of one color. Example: blue 4, 5, 6. A 1 is low; 12, 13, 1 is invalid.

    Opening: play one or more new sets worth at least 30 points, entirely from your rack. Table manipulation becomes available on your following turns.

    Click to move: click tiles to select or deselect them. Choose New set, or click Add selected here beside a table set. You can temporarily leave an invalid set while rearranging; everything must be valid when you end your turn.

    Rearranging: after opening, select tiles from existing sets and move them into other sets. You may split runs or rebuild groups. Every old table tile must remain on the table, and you must add at least one tile from your rack.

    Jokers: a star substitutes for a needed tile. Its small label shows its current meaning. If more than one meaning is possible, use the set's dropdown. You may free and reuse a table joker after opening, but it must end this turn in a valid set; it cannot enter your rack.

    Drawing: draw one tile when you cannot or choose not to play. Drawing ends your turn; you cannot immediately play the drawn tile.

    Clock: the default is 60 seconds. Timeout restores the start-of-turn layout and draws one tile. An unfinished invalid arrangement at timeout instead draws three penalty tiles. Practice mode removes the time limit. The clock continues if you switch browser tabs.

    Following the action: the floating panel keeps your timer and the latest action in view. The computer thinks for 4–8 seconds, then shows its play in steps. Your clock starts after its move finishes. NEW marks your latest draw, BOT marks tiles the computer added, and MOVED marks table tiles it rearranged. Highlights remain during your next turn, including after sorting.

    End and scoring: emptying your rack wins the game. The other rack's numbers are added up, with each remaining joker worth 30; the winner gains that total and the loser loses it. If the pool is empty and both players pass, continue if a move remains. Otherwise use Finish blocked game; the lower rack total wins the difference.

    Suggest move: stages a possible play for you to review. Click End turn to accept it, or Undo to continue your own arrangement. The computer uses the same search and cannot see your rack.
